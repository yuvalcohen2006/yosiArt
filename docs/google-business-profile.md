# Google Business Profile — YosiArt

Everything to paste into a Google Business Profile for Yosi Cohen / YosiArt.
Each block below is copy-paste ready. Field-length limits are noted where Google
enforces one.

Set up at **business.google.com** with the same Google account already verified
in Search Console.

---

## 0. Read this first — eligibility

Google's guidelines say a business qualifies for a profile only if it **makes
in-person contact with customers**. A business that operates *entirely* online
is not eligible.

YosiArt is borderline, and the site's own accessibility statement says in
writing: *"the business has no physical premises open to the public."* Do not
contradict that — listing a storefront address would be both a guideline
violation and inconsistent with published legal text.

**The correct setup is a service-area business:**

- When asked *"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"* → **No**
- Enter the home/studio address only on the verification screen (Google needs it
  to post the verification card); then tick **"Hide my address"**
- Define a service area instead (see §5)

This is honest and legitimate: works are handed over or delivered in person, and
commissions involve meeting the client. If Google rejects the listing anyway,
that is not a failure of the site — the SEO work in the launch guide stands on
its own.

## 10. Products (optional — recommend skipping)

The site deliberately shows **no prices**; the price is revealed only on the
painting page and every sale starts as a conversation. Google's Products
section pushes hard for a price or a price range, and a profile full of
"Price on request" entries reads worse than no products at all.

**Recommendation: skip Products. Use Services (§9) plus Photos (§11).**

If you do want a few products anyway, add three or four flagship pieces in this
shape, and set the price to the real number rather than a placeholder:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Product name | Painting title, e.g. `Allied Skies` |
| Category | `Original Paintings` |
| Price | The real price, or leave blank |
| Description | The English description from the painting's page on the site |
| Link | `https://yosiart.com/work/<slug>` |

The description text for every painting is already written and lives on each
painting's page — copy it from there so the wording matches the site exactly.

## 11. Photos

Photos drive more profile engagement than anything else here. Upload in this
order:

1. **Logo** — square, 720×720 or larger. Use the site's favicon artwork.
2. **Cover** — 1200×630, landscape. The `og-default.jpg` in `public/` is exactly
   this size and already optimised.
3. **Profile / "team"** — the portrait of Yosi at `public/assets/dad.jpeg`.
4. **At work** — stills pulled from the studio video (`painter-loop.mp4`).
   Hands, brushes, paint, an unfinished canvas. These outperform finished-work
   shots because they prove a real person makes the thing.
5. **10–15 paintings** — pull the highest-resolution originals from the Sanity
   library, at least two from each of the six collections.

Re-upload something every few weeks. Google measurably favours profiles that
keep receiving fresh photos.

## 13. Q&A — seed these yourself

Google lets the owner post questions *and* answer them. Do this on day one, or
strangers will fill the space instead. Post each question from a friend's or
family member's Google account, then answer from the business account.

**Q:** האם היצירות מקוריות או הדפסים?
**A:** כל היצירות מקוריות לחלוטין, מצוירות ביד וחתומות. אין הדפסים, אין עותקים, וכל ציור נמכר פעם אחת בלבד.

**Q:** אפשר להזמין ציור בהתאמה אישית?
**A:** בהחלט. יוסי מקבל הזמנות לדיוקנים וליצירות המותאמות לחלל מסוים. התהליך מתחיל בשיחה על הנושא, הגודל וגוון הצבעים. אפשר לפנות בוואטסאפ או בדוא"ל.

**Q:** איך רוכשים ציור? יש קנייה ישירה באתר?
**A:** אין תשלום ישיר באתר. כל ציור נמכר בפנייה אישית — וואטסאפ, דוא"ל או טלפון — ומשם מסכמים מחיר, תשלום ואספקה ישירות מול האמן.

**Q:** יש משלוח מחוץ לאזור המרכז?
**A:** כן. יש משלוח לכל הארץ, ארוז בקפידה ומתואם מראש. למשלוח לחו"ל אפשר לקבל הצעת מחיר.

**Q:** באילו טכניקות יוסי עובד?
**A:** בעיקר אקריליק על קנבס, וגם רישומים בעפרונות צבעוניים. הסגנון נע בין היפר-ריאליזם לאבסטרקט, ולרוב משלב בין השניים באותה יצירה.

## 14. Reviews

Reviews are the strongest local-ranking signal there is, and a profile with zero
reviews ranks nowhere. Once the profile is live, Google gives you a short share
link (`Get more reviews` on the dashboard).

Send this to past buyers on WhatsApp:

```
היי! שמחתי מאוד שהיצירה הגיעה אליך.
פתחתי עכשיו עמוד עסק בגוגל, ואם יש לך רגע — חוות דעת קצרה שם תעזור לי המון שאנשים ימצאו את העבודות.
הקישור: [PASTE THE GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
תודה רבה 🙏
```

Never offer anything in exchange for a review — discounts, freebies, or entry
into a draw. Google removes incentivised reviews and can suspend the profile.

## 15. After it goes live

- **Verification** takes 5–14 days by postcard in Israel; video verification is
  sometimes offered and is much faster — take it if offered.
- **Link it to Search Console** — same Google account, so the two data sets sit
  together.
- **Post weekly.** A profile that stops posting slides down local results.
- **Answer every review** within a few days, including the good ones.
- **Never edit the business name** to add keywords, however tempting.

## Note on Sanity

None of the content above lives in Sanity, and none of it should. Google
Business Profile content is entered in Google's own dashboard — there is no API
path from this repo to that profile.

For the record, the `siteSettings` singleton in Sanity is currently empty
(the document does not exist), and `SITE_SETTINGS_QUERY` in
`src/sanity/queries.ts` is defined but never called by any component. The bio,
contact details, and social links the site actually renders are hardcoded in
`src/pages/About.tsx` and `src/lib/contact.ts`. So populating `siteSettings`
today would change nothing on the live site — it is dead scaffolding for a
milestone that was never wired up.
